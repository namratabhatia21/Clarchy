# Open-source project logos

Diagrams of the open-source design show each project's own logo, so a reader recognises
PostgreSQL, Kafka or Keycloak at a glance. The logos are trademarks of their projects or
the organisations behind them, are not covered by Clarchy's licence, and are used only to
refer to those projects, the way documentation and architecture diagrams commonly do.
Every diagram says service names and icons belong to their owners and that Clarchy is not
affiliated with them. Projects with no logo here keep a lettered badge.

Most files come from Simple Icons 16.33.0 (<https://simpleicons.org>, npm `simple-icons`),
whose collection is published under CC0 while each logo stays its owner's; its
single-colour shapes are filled here with the brand colour Simple Icons records for them.
Licence notes Simple Icons gives for a logo are listed below; check each project's
guidelines before using a logo elsewhere.
