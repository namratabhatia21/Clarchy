# SnapNest photo sharing

SnapNest is a mobile app and website where families share photo albums privately.

- Around 50k monthly active users at launch, mostly in India.
- Users sign up with email or Google and invite relatives to albums.
- People upload photos and short videos from their phones; we create thumbnails in the background and send a push notification when an album changes.
- Albums, comments and likes are simple records looked up by album id.
- Traffic is spiky: busy on weekends and festivals, quiet at night.
- About 2 TB of media in the first year.
